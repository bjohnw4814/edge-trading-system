The Million Dollar Plan — overview

A fluid roadmap for building real wealth off prop-firm accounts, slow and steady, without betting it on any one firm's rules staying the same. It's a living document — firms change their rules, so the specifics get monitored and updated over time. Three versions of the plan follow. Plan A rotates early, banking gains before any real trigger fires — smoother, but it leaves some payout runway on the table. Plan B rides each firm to its actual limit before moving on — squeezes more out of each account, but rides closer to the real trigger lines, including on the firms with weaker reputations. Plan C optimizes for something different entirely: the fastest, cheapest path to a first payout — useful as a quick proof-of-concept, not a replacement for the long-term core.

The cascade, step by step

Ten prop-firm accounts, activated one at a time, each carrying the next. Figures below are for a $50K Lucid Pro Evaluation account.

1

Start with one account in Evaluation

Account #1 begins in Eval status. Every account after it starts as "Not Started" until it's unlocked.

2

Hit your daily goal

Every active account shares the same dollar target each trading day, based on how many accounts you've unlocked so far — see the table below. Miss it or beat it, the cumulative profit is what matters.

3

Graduate at $3,000

Once an account's cumulative profit reaches $3,000, it graduates from Eval to Funded. This happens once per account, the first time through.

4

First payout at $4,100, then $2,500 each

Once funded, that account's first payout needs $4,100 cumulative profit and pays out $2,000. Every payout after that only needs $2,500 cumulative profit and pays $2,500. Reaching an account's very first payout is what unlocks the next account in line — though you still have to go add it yourself.

5

Maxed out after the 4th payout

After its 4th payout, that account is maxed out and marked Resting while you go purchase a fresh one. It's capped one payout short of Lucid's own 5-payout live-transition trigger on purpose — taking a 5th payout moves that account onto its live-funded track and closes every other simulated account on the household, which would end the cascade. The new account starts the whole process over: evaluate up to $3,000 to get funded, $4,100 for its first $2,000 payout, then $2,500 for every payout after that. Rinse and repeat.

6

Repeat, ten accounts deep

Each account you add compounds the ones before it. By the time all ten are unlocked, you're copy-trading your daily goal across the full cascade.
